## 4. PHOTO STRATEGY: CADENCE, QUALITY & GEOTAGGING REALITY

### The Geotagging Myth vs. Reality
> [!NOTE]
> **Debunking the Geotagging Myth:** Many outdated SEO guides advise running EXIF geotag injection software over images before uploading them to GBP. **Google strips all EXIF metadata upon upload.** Google’s computer vision detects authenticity via:
> 1. Visual object recognition (actual buses, registration plates, clean upholstery, branded uniforms).
> 2. Native device GPS coordinates captured by an authentic mobile device during upload via Google Maps app.
> 3. Natural user-generated uploads from local customer Google accounts who have location history enabled.

### Upload Schedule & Shot List (Cadence: 2–3 new photos every week)
1. **Vehicle Exterior Shots (3/4 Angle):** Crisp front-and-side photos showing clean bodywork and commercial yellow registration plates in broad daylight.
2. **Interior Comfort Shots:** Clear photos showcasing wide legroom, pristine pushback fabric, functioning overhead AC louvers, and ambient ceiling lights.
3. **Driver Professionalism:** Photos of uniformed drivers conducting pre-trip inspections, cleaning windows, or loading customer luggage.
4. **Landmark Context Shots:** Vehicles parked safely at recognizable regional landmarks (e.g., outside BBI Airport terminal, Puri Grand Road bus bay, Dhauli Shanti Stupa parking).
