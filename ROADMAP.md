# SEO ROADMAP & MEASUREMENT PLAN: RENTANYBUS.COM

## 1. OBJECTIVES & MEASURABLE TARGETS (KPIs)

* **Organic Search Clicks:** Grow from baseline to **5,000+ monthly non-branded clicks** within 90 days.
* **Organic Search Impressions:** Reach **100,000+ monthly impressions** across target Odisha commercial queries.
* **Top 3 Rankings:** Rank in the top 3 on Google for 15+ primary commercial keywords (`bus rental bhubaneswar`, `tempo traveller hire bhubaneswar`, `wedding bus rental cuttack`, `puri tour bus hire`).
* **Core Web Vitals:** Maintain **90+ Lighthouse Mobile Performance** and 100% Good status for LCP (<2.5s), INP (<200ms), and CLS (<0.1).
* **Organic Lead Conversions:** Generate **150+ high-intent booking leads / direct phone calls** per month from organic search.

---

## 2. 30 / 60 / 90 DAY EXECUTION ROADMAP

### PHASE 1 (Days 1–30): Technical SEO Foundation & Indexation
* **Week 1:**
  * Deploy `<SEOHead />` across all routes with unique titles, meta descriptions, and canonicals.
  * Generate and publish compliant `sitemap.xml` with all public indexable routes.
  * Fix `robots.txt` with Sitemap reference and disallow rules for `/admin*` and private routes.
  * Deploy branded `og-image.png` (1200×630px) to eliminate social preview 404s.
* **Week 2:**
  * Inject Google-compliant JSON-LD schemas: `LocalBusiness`, `Organization`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `Product/Offer`.
  * Standardize NAP details (`+91 82495 29220`, Bhubaneswar, Odisha) across all footer, header, and schema properties.
  * Google Search Console (GSC) sitemap submission & Bing Webmaster verification.
* **Week 3:**
  * Build programmatic local city hub templates (`/bus-rental/bhubaneswar`, `/bus-rental/puri`, `/bus-rental/cuttack`).
  * Integrate breadcrumb trails and internal link mesh from homepage and footer.
* **Week 4:**
  * Image optimization pass: verify next-gen WebP formats, explicit width/height dimensions to prevent layout shifts (CLS), and keyword-rich alt attributes.
  * Performance audit and code splitting optimization.

---

### PHASE 2 (Days 31–60): Programmatic Hubs & Local Authority
* **Weeks 5–6:**
  * Expand city hubs to Balasore, Berhampur, Rourkela, and Sambalpur.
  * Launch top intercity route landing pages:
    * `/routes/bhubaneswar-to-puri`
    * `/routes/bhubaneswar-to-konark`
    * `/routes/bhubaneswar-to-chilika`
    * `/routes/cuttack-to-puri`
  * Add distance (km), route duration, toll costs, and vehicle suitability breakdowns to route pages.
* **Weeks 7–8:**
  * Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization: Complete primary category (`Bus tour agency` / `Bus charter service`), upload real high-res fleet photos, add products/services, and seed automated post-trip review generation via WhatsApp SMS.
  * Local directory and citation building across 25+ trusted Indian portals (JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, YellowPages India, Odisha Tourism forums).

---

### PHASE 3 (Days 61–90): Helpful Content, FAQ Snippet Dominance & PR
* **Weeks 9–10:**
  * Publish in-depth commercial pricing and planning guides:
    * *"Bus Rental Cost per Km in Odisha: Complete 2026 Price Guide"*
    * *"How to Choose the Right Bus Size for an Indian Wedding"*
    * *"Step-by-Step Guide for Booking a Jagannath Dham Puri Yatra Bus"*
  * Capture Google "People Also Ask" (PAA) rich snippets using embedded schema-backed FAQs.
* **Weeks 11–12:**
  * Local B2B and institutional outreach: Establish partner resource links with Bhubaneswar wedding planners, event organizers, school boards, and corporate travel desks.
  * Full ranking and crawl audit review; iterate based on GSC performance queries and search impression data.
